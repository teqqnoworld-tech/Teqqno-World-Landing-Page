Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about minimum orders, timelines, and sample availability.

What printing and branding services do you offer? expand_more
We offer end-to-end commercial solutions: offset & digital printing (cards, brochures, flyers, bill books), promotional apparel (custom polo shirts, caps), exhibition hardware (roll-up standees, banners, signboards), premium executive gifting sets, and turnkey brand identity design.
Can I request customized products and physical samples? expand_more
Yes, absolutely. For bulk corporate orders, we can dispatch physical paper stock swatches, fabric samples, and blank hardware prototypes to your office, or prepare a pre-production sample proof before mass batch printing.
How can I get a quote and what is the typical turnaround time? expand_more
You can submit our quick quote form or ping us on WhatsApp (+91 8309394934). Standard print orders ship within 2 to 4 business days. Express emergency same-day/next-day turnaround is also available for standees, banners, and digital flyers across Hyderabad.
Do you provide corporate gifts and promotional merchandise in bulk? expand_more
Yes, our corporate merchandising division handles bulk orders from 50 units to 25,000+ units. We manage laser engraving, UV printing, screen printing, custom rigid packaging, and multi-location logistics for large teams.
How can I contact your team or visit your office in Hyderabad? expand_more
Our registered facility is located at Sri Datta Plaza, Plot No. 14, Ameenpur Road, Behind HDFC Bank, Padmaja Nagar, Suraksha Colony, Bandam Kommu, Chanda Nagar, Hyderabad. We welcome client visits Monday through Saturday, from 9:30 AM to 8:30 PM.
